The fastest way to get better at AI video prompts is to look at prompts that work, then change them for your own idea. Reading theory helps, but seeing the same structure used for a skincare ad, a food clip and a mountain sunrise makes it click.

Below are 30 AI video prompt examples sorted by category. Each one is written to work in most current models, like Seedance, Kling, Veo and Sora. After each category, there's a short note on why the prompts work, so you can write your own.

## The pattern behind every example

Almost every prompt below follows the same order:

1. **Shot and camera.** Shot size and one camera move.
2. **Subject.** Who or what, with one or two specific details.
3. **Action.** One clear thing that happens.
4. **Scene and light.** Where it is and what the light is like.
5. **Style.** The look.
6. **Sound.** What we hear, including "no dialogue" or "no music" when needed.

If you want the full explanation of each part, read [how to write AI video prompts](/blog/how-to-write-ai-video-prompts). If you just want to copy and go, keep reading.

## UGC and talking to camera

**1. Product review**

> Medium close-up at eye level, static. A woman in her late 20s with her hair in a loose bun sits on a sofa and holds a small blue water bottle up to the camera. She taps the time markers on the side and says, "See these lines? They tell you if you're behind on water by lunchtime." Natural daylight from a side window, a lived-in living room behind her. Realistic, unpolished phone video look. Audio: her voice, quiet room tone. No subtitles.

**2. Morning routine**

> Medium shot, handheld. A man in his 30s in a grey t-shirt stands in a small kitchen, pours coffee into a mug and turns to the camera, smiling. He says, "Okay, two minutes, here's my whole morning." Soft morning light, a bit of clutter on the counter. Real home video feel. Audio: coffee pouring, his voice. No subtitles.

**3. Car talk**

> Close-up from the dashboard, static. A young woman in a denim jacket sits in the driver's seat of a parked car and leans toward the camera. She says quietly, "I have to tell you what happened at work today." Daylight through the windshield, a car park outside. Natural, slightly imperfect phone footage. Audio: her voice, muffled traffic outside. No subtitles.

**4. Unboxing**

> Close-up, overhead, static. Two hands open a plain brown box on a wooden table, pull back tissue paper and lift out a pair of white headphones. Soft natural light from a window. Realistic, crisp phone video. Audio: cardboard opening, paper rustling, a small "oh nice". No subtitles. No music.

**Why these work:** they look like real phone footage because the prompts ask for natural light, ordinary rooms and small imperfections. Short dialogue in quotes gives the model exact words to say. For a full workflow, see our guide on [how to make AI UGC ads](/blog/how-to-make-ai-ugc-ads).

## Product shots

**5. Splash shot**

> Extreme close-up, slow push-in. A can of sparkling lemon drink sits on wet black stone as cold water splashes over it, droplets running down the sides. Dark background, one soft light from above. Clean, premium commercial style. Audio: water splashing, a soft fizz. No dialogue.

**6. Floating product**

> Close-up, slow orbit. A white running shoe floats and slowly turns in mid-air, laces lifting slightly. Plain light grey studio background, soft even light. Minimal commercial style. Audio: a soft whoosh, a light ambient hum. No dialogue.

**7. In use**

> Medium close-up, static. A hand presses the button on a small black blender, it whirs to life and turns strawberries and milk into a pink smoothie. Bright kitchen counter, morning light. Realistic, clean look. Audio: the blender motor, ice cracking. No dialogue. No music.

**8. Before and after**

> Wide shot, static. A messy desk covered in cables, then a pair of hands clips the cables into a black tray under the desk, and the desk is left clean and tidy. Natural daylight, a home office. Realistic, satisfying look. Audio: clips snapping, cables sliding. No dialogue.

**Why these work:** one product, one action, one camera move. Product shots look premium when the background is simple and the light is controlled, so the prompts say exactly that.

## Food and drink

**9. Cheese pull**

> Extreme close-up, slow pull-back. A hand lifts a slice of pizza from the pan and a long string of melted cheese stretches down. Warm kitchen light, steam rising. Mouth-watering food commercial look. Audio: a soft sizzle, cheese stretching. No dialogue.

**10. Pour shot**

> Close-up, static. Iced coffee is poured into a tall glass full of ice, then milk swirls in and slowly mixes. A cafe counter, soft daylight from the side. Clean, appetising look. Audio: ice cracking, liquid pouring. No dialogue. No music.

**11. Street food**

> Medium shot, handheld. A street vendor flips noodles in a large wok over a high flame at night, steam and smoke rising into the lights. A busy night market behind him. Documentary style, natural colour. Audio: sizzling, the wok scraping, crowd noise. No dialogue.

**Why these work:** food clips sell texture, steam and movement. The sound line matters more than you'd think. A sizzle makes the picture feel real.

## Cinematic and nature

**12. Mountain sunrise**

> Aerial shot, slow forward movement. Sunrise over a mountain range, mist filling the valleys below, the first light hitting the peaks. Calm, epic nature documentary style. Audio: soft wind, distant birds. No dialogue.

**13. Rainy window**

> Extreme close-up, shallow depth of field. A young man rests his head on a bus window at night, city lights blurring past, rain running down the glass. Cool blue tones, quiet and thoughtful mood. Audio: rain on the window, the bus engine humming. No dialogue.

**14. Ocean**

> Low angle, static. A wave curls and crashes onto black volcanic rocks, white spray bursting into the air. Overcast sky, moody light. Dramatic nature film style. Audio: the wave crashing, wind. No dialogue.

**15. City night**

> Wide shot, slow crane up. A busy city crossing at night after rain, people with umbrellas crossing in every direction, neon signs reflected in the wet road. Cinematic, rich colour. Audio: traffic, footsteps, light rain. No dialogue.

**Why these work:** big scenes need a clear camera move to feel cinematic. One mood word ("calm", "moody", "thoughtful") steers the whole look.

## Faceless b-roll for voiceover videos

These are made to sit under a voiceover, so they ask for no dialogue. If you run a faceless channel, see [how to start a faceless YouTube channel with AI](/blog/faceless-youtube-channel-with-ai).

**16. Old library**

> Slow dolly forward. An old library with tall wooden shelves full of leather books, dust drifting in beams of light from high windows. Warm, historical documentary look. Audio: faint creaking, soft room tone. No dialogue.

**17. Money stress**

> Close-up, static. A man's hands spread unpaid bills across a kitchen table under a single lamp, then he rubs his forehead. Dim evening light. Realistic drama style. Audio: paper sliding, a clock ticking. No dialogue.

**18. Laptop work**

> Over the shoulder, static. A woman types on a laptop in a quiet cafe, a spreadsheet on the screen, a half-finished coffee beside her. Soft daylight. Realistic, natural look. Audio: keyboard typing, cafe chatter. No dialogue.

**19. Ancient city**

> Aerial shot, slow orbit. A walled stone city in the desert at sunset, smoke rising from rooftops, people moving through narrow streets. Golden light, historical epic style. Audio: wind, distant voices, animals. No dialogue.

**20. Space**

> Slow push-in. The Earth seen from orbit at night, city lights glowing along the coastlines, a thin blue line of atmosphere at the edge. Realistic, awe-inspiring documentary look. Audio: a low, quiet hum. No dialogue.

**21. Running against time**

> Tracking shot. A young man in a suit runs through a train station, checking his watch, dodging people as the departure board flips. Bright station lights. Realistic, tense mood. Audio: footsteps, station announcements, a train horn. No dialogue.

**Why these work:** each clip shows one clear idea that a voiceover line can sit on. Saying "no dialogue" stops characters from talking over your voice.

## Travel and real estate

**22. Villa reveal**

> Slow dolly forward through open glass doors into a bright modern living room with a sea view, white sofas and wooden floors. Late afternoon sun. Clean real estate video style. Audio: soft breeze, distant waves. No dialogue. No music.

**23. Old town walk**

> Handheld walking shot. A narrow cobbled street in an old European town in the morning, flower boxes on the windows, a cafe owner setting out chairs. Soft natural light. Travel vlog style. Audio: footsteps, chairs scraping, birds. No dialogue.

**24. Beach drone**

> Aerial shot, slow pull-back. A small boat anchored in clear turquoise water next to a white sand beach and palm trees. Bright midday sun. Travel ad style. Audio: gentle waves, wind. No dialogue.

**Why these work:** travel and property clips sell a feeling of being there, so the camera moves through the space instead of looking at it from far away.

## Animation and stylised

**25. Clay animation**

> Medium shot, static. A small clay fox in a knitted scarf builds a snowman in a snowy forest, then steps back proudly. Handmade stop-motion clay style, soft daylight. Audio: crunching snow, a tiny happy squeak. No dialogue.

**26. Paper cut-out**

> Wide shot, slow pan right. A paper cut-out city at night, layered buildings with tiny glowing windows, a paper moon rising. Handmade paper craft style. Audio: soft wind, a distant train. No dialogue.

**27. 3D cartoon**

> Medium shot, static. A round orange robot with big eyes tries to water a plant, misses, and soaks itself, then shakes off the water. Bright, colourful 3D cartoon style, soft studio light. Audio: water splashing, a short robotic beep. No dialogue.

**Why these work:** naming one clear style ("clay", "paper craft", "3D cartoon") gives the model a strong look to follow. Simple actions keep animation clean.

## Funny and viral moments

**28. The cat and the cup**

> Close-up, static. A ginger cat on a kitchen counter stares at a full glass of water, slowly pushes it toward the edge with one paw, then looks straight at the camera. Natural home light. Realistic phone video look. Audio: the glass sliding. No dialogue.

**29. Gym fail**

> Medium shot, handheld. A man confidently steps onto a treadmill that's already running fast, stumbles, grabs the handles and pretends nothing happened. A busy gym. Realistic phone video look. Audio: the treadmill whirring, a few people laughing. No subtitles.

**30. Wrong order**

> Medium close-up, static. A woman opens a takeaway bag at her desk, pulls out a tiny salad instead of the burger she ordered, and stares at the camera in silence. Office lighting. Realistic, deadpan comedy style. Audio: the paper bag rustling, office noise. No dialogue.

**Why these work:** funny clips need one clear setup and one payoff, plus a reaction shot or look to camera. The realistic phone look makes them feel like real moments.

## How to change these for your model

The prompts above work in most models, but each one has its own strengths:

- **Seedance:** start with the shot, add timed beats like "[0-3s]" for longer clips. See our [Seedance prompt guide](/blog/seedance-prompt-guide).
- **Kling:** describe motion step by step and use one precise camera move per shot. See our [Kling prompt guide](/blog/kling-ai-prompt-guide).
- **Veo:** camera first, dialogue in quotes and "SFX:" labels for sound. See our [Veo 3.1 prompt guide](/blog/veo-3-prompt-guide).

If you want the same character in several clips, add a reference image rather than describing their face in text. Our guide on [AI character consistency](/blog/ai-character-consistency) explains how.

## Common mistakes when copying prompts

- **Changing the subject but not the sound.** A cafe prompt with "waves crashing" in the audio line will confuse the model.
- **Adding a second camera move.** One per shot.
- **Making the dialogue too long.** About 2 to 3 words per second of clip.
- **Stacking style words.** "Cinematic, realistic, anime, vintage" fight each other. Pick one look.

## FAQ

### Do these prompts work in every AI video model?

Most of them work as they are in Seedance, Kling, Veo, Sora and similar models. For the best results, adjust the format to your model, like timed beats for Seedance or labelled shots for Kling.

### How long should an AI video prompt be?

For most clips, 40 to 150 words is enough. Longer prompts are fine for multi-shot clips, but every extra detail competes for the model's attention.

### Why does my AI video add captions or music I didn't ask for?

Many models add them by default. End your prompt with "No subtitles" and "No music" when you don't want them.

### Can I use these prompts for commercial videos?

You can use and change these prompts freely. Check the terms of the AI video tool you use for commercial use, and follow each platform's rules on labelling realistic AI content.
